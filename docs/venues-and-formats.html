01 · Where we stand

What we are

Affordable nights in the centre of Perugia, good music, an open bar, and a room where people from Umbria and from everywhere else actually meet. Not a nightclub, not a private house party any more.

What is fixed

RhythmAbout once a month
Size80+ guests, demand is higher
BarOpen bar, always
ThemeEvery edition has one
Age18+, photo ID
Crew9 people, free entry, small fee from Dec

What has to change

No permanent location so far, and the last edition was not fully in order on the legal side. From now on: the venue carries the licence, everything agreed in writing before the night, and the numbers settled the week after.

03 · The three formats

Classic PerugiaParty

Late · 22:00 to close
The nightTheme, open bar, two DJs, dancing from the first hour. Who comesStudents and Erasmus, 80 to 150. We needRoom to dance, a real bar, sound, late closing, neighbours who can take it. TicketEarly bird / normal / late, open bar included.

Cena with live music

Early · 20:00 to 00:00
The nightSeated dinner, one live act or acoustic set, drinks after. Cultural, calmer, still ours. Who comesMixed ages, locals as well as students, 40 to 80 seated. We needA kitchen, tables, a corner for the band, a venue that is proud of its food. TicketOne price, dinner and the act included, drinks separate or capped.

Aperitivo

Early · 18:30 to 22:00
The nightDrink, buffet, DJ set on low volume. The easy way in for people who do not know us. Who comesWalk-ins and first-timers, 50 to 100, high turnover. We needOutdoor or a street-facing room, fast bar, simple food. TicketLow fixed price, drink plus buffet, no theme needed.
One venue rarely does all three

04 · What we ask of a venue

Non negotiable

  • 01Licence and public liability in the venue's name
  • 02Capacity on paper, not by eye
  • 03Closing time in writing
  • 04Open bar possible, price agreed per guest
  • 05SIAE and music handled, and by whom
  • 06Our own DJs and our own decoration allowed

Makes it good

  • 07Walking distance from the centre
  • 08A real dance floor, not a corridor
  • 09Own sound, or room for ours
  • 10Kitchen, if we want the dinner format
  • 11Outdoor space for the aperitivo
  • 12Staff who have done a busy night before
