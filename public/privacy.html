Privacy.

This is the complete information notice under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the General Data Protection Regulation, “GDPR”), Legislative Decree no. 196 of 30 June 2003 as amended by Legislative Decree no. 101 of 10 August 2018 (the Italian “Codice in materia di protezione dei dati personali”), and the guidelines of the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) on cookies and other tracking tools of 10 June 2021. To exercise your rights or to have your data deleted, send a direct message to @perugiaparty on Instagram (see section 8).

1. Who is responsible for your data (data controller)

The data controller is Mattheo van der Lee, who organises the independent student events known as “PerugiaParty” in Perugia, Italy (“PerugiaParty”, “we”, “us”). PerugiaParty is run by volunteers and is not a club, venue or nightlife company. You can contact the controller for any question about this notice or about your personal data by sending a direct message to the Instagram account @perugiaparty (https://ig.me/m/perugiaparty). Requests received this way are handled by the controller personally. We have not appointed a data protection officer, because the processing we carry out does not require one under Article 37 GDPR.

2. Which personal data we process

We only process the data needed to organise private events with a guest list. Depending on how you interact with us, this may include the following categories of personal data.

2.1 Request data. When you request a place through the form on perugiaparty.com (or on its Italian and Erasmus versions), we collect your full name, your Instagram username, your WhatsApp or telephone number if you choose to give it, the optional message you write to us, your confirmation that you are at least 18 years old and that you accept the house rules, and the date and time of the request. We also assign a personal request code (for example “H26-XXXXXX”) and a personal link to your ticket page.

2.2 Verification data. To confirm that the Instagram account given in the request really belongs to you, we ask you to send your personal code to @perugiaparty by direct message. When you do, we receive from Meta Platforms the Instagram username of the sending account, an internal Instagram identifier for that account (a page-scoped ID), the content of your message and the time it was sent. We use these to match the message to your request and to reply to you.

2.3 Ticket and event data. For each event, we record the status of your request (requested, on the waiting list, approved, paid, checked in, declined or removed), the type and price of your ticket, any voluntary contribution you choose to add, the payment deadline, whether you have attended previous events (to give returning guests priority), your ticket code and QR code, and the time at which you are checked in at the door.

2.4 Payment data. Payments are made by bank transfer to our bank account. We see the information your bank sends with the transfer (normally the name of the account holder, the amount, the date and the payment reference) in our own banking application, and we use it only to match your payment to your ticket. This website does not collect or store bank account numbers, card numbers or other payment credentials.

2.5 Technical and approximate location data. When you use the website, our hosting provider necessarily processes your IP address and basic technical information (such as browser type and the time of the request) in order to deliver the pages. When you send a request, we store the approximate city and country the request came from, as derived by our hosting provider from your connection; we do not store your IP address together with your request. To protect the website from abuse, we keep a short-lived counter of requests linked to a one-way, salted and truncated hash of your IP address; this counter cannot be used to recover the IP address and is deleted automatically, normally within one hour.

2.6 Data stored on your device. The website stores a small amount of information in your own browser (localStorage), such as your personal ticket link and the details you typed in the form, so that you can return to your ticket without typing them again. If you install the website as an app or allow notifications, your browser also stores a technical subscription that lets us send you updates about your own ticket. This information stays on your device and you can delete it at any time from your browser settings.

2.7 Photo pages. After an event, guests who signed up can open a page with a link to the event photos for a limited time, by logging in with their Instagram username and personal code. We record the access only to the extent technically necessary to check that you were on the guest list.

2.8 Photographs and videos at events. Photographs and short videos may be taken at our events and shared with guests or on our Instagram account. If you do not want to appear, tell a member of the crew at the event or send us a direct message, and we will avoid photographing you or remove the content where possible.

2.9 Crew members. If you volunteer as crew, we also process the data needed to coordinate the event: your name, username, password (stored only as a salted cryptographic hash), Instagram username, the tasks and purchases assigned to you, expenses you submit for reimbursement, your attendance and shifts, and the notes and messages you add to the crew dashboard.

We do not ask for, and ask you not to send us, special categories of data within the meaning of Article 9 GDPR (for example data about health, religion or sexual orientation). We do not check or store identity document numbers: a photo ID is only looked at by the door staff to confirm your name and age, and no copy is made.

3. Purposes and legal bases of the processing

We process your personal data for the following purposes, each on the legal basis indicated.

(a) Managing your request and your participation in an event, including reviewing requests, verifying your Instagram account, communicating your price, managing the waiting list, issuing your ticket, matching your payment and checking you in at the door. Legal basis: the performance of a contract to which you are a party, or steps taken at your request before entering into it (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

(b) Verifying that guests are at least 18 years old and that each account is real, and keeping our events safe, including refusing or removing guests who break the house rules. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in organising safe, private events for adults and in preventing fake or duplicate requests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

(c) Giving priority to returning guests and recognising you at later events. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in rewarding guests who have attended before and in managing limited places fairly (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

(d) Protecting the website against spam, automated requests and misuse, including the request counter described in section 2.5 and, where enabled, a human verification check. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in the security and integrity of the website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

(e) Understanding, in aggregate, where our guests come from (city and country). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in planning events (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not create individual profiles for this purpose.

(f) Sending you service messages about your request and your ticket by Instagram direct message, WhatsApp or browser notification (for example approval, payment reminders and the location of the event). Legal basis: performance of the contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for browser notifications, your consent given through your browser (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by switching notifications off.

(g) Keeping records of payments and expenses where required by applicable tax and accounting rules. Legal basis: compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

(h) Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims. Legal basis: our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Where we rely on legitimate interest, we have balanced our interest against your rights and freedoms and consider that the processing is limited, expected by guests of a private event and not intrusive. You may object to it at any time (see section 8).

4. Is providing your data required?

Giving your name, your Instagram username and your age confirmation is necessary to request a place: without them we cannot review your request or issue a ticket. Your WhatsApp number and your message are optional; if you do not give them, we will only contact you on Instagram. Sending your code by Instagram direct message is necessary to confirm your request.

5. Who receives your data

Your data is accessed only by the controller and, to the extent needed for their task, by the volunteer crew members who help run the event (for example the door staff, who see your name, ticket status and check-in time). Crew members are bound to confidentiality and act under our instructions. Money amounts and payment data are visible only to the controller.

We also use the following service providers, who process data on our behalf as data processors under Article 28 GDPR, or as independent controllers for their own services, in each case under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (website hosting, content delivery, database, security and, where enabled, the “Turnstile” human verification check). Cloudflare processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the website.
  • Meta Platforms Ireland Limited and Meta Platforms, Inc. (Instagram direct messages and, if you contact us there, WhatsApp). Messages you exchange with us on these services are also processed by Meta under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC (storage of the event photo albums linked from the photos page, and delivery of browser notifications on some devices).
  • Apple Inc. and Mozilla Corporation (delivery of browser notifications on their browsers, if you allow notifications).
  • Our bank, which processes bank transfers you make to us under its own terms.

We may also disclose data to public authorities where required by law. We never sell your personal data and we never share it with third parties for their own marketing.

6. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Some of the providers listed in section 5 are based in, or use servers in, the United States and other countries outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens, the transfer is based on the European Commission’s adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for providers certified under it, or on the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission (Article 46(2)(c) GDPR), together with the additional safeguards those providers apply. You can obtain more information about these safeguards by contacting us.

7. How long we keep your data

Request, verification and ticket data are kept for as long as we organise events, so that we can recognise returning guests and give them priority, unless you ask us to delete them earlier. Guest lists of past events are reviewed and cleaned up regularly. Payment and expense records are kept for as long as required by applicable tax and accounting rules, normally up to ten years. The request counter described in section 2.5 is deleted automatically, normally within one hour. Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it. Instagram and WhatsApp conversations remain on those services under Meta’s retention rules; we delete our own copies when you ask us to delete your data. Data needed to establish or defend legal claims may be kept for the applicable limitation period.

8. Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 22 GDPR you have the right to: obtain confirmation of whether we process your data and a copy of it (right of access); have inaccurate data corrected (right to rectification); have your data deleted (right to erasure, “right to be forgotten”); have the processing restricted (right to restriction); receive the data you gave us in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and have it transmitted to another controller (right to data portability); and object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on our legitimate interest (right to object). Where processing is based on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of the processing carried out before. We do not use automated decision-making, including profiling, that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR: every request is reviewed by a person.

To exercise your rights, send a direct message to @perugiaparty on Instagram stating your request and your Instagram username. We may ask for information to confirm your identity. We will reply without undue delay and in any case within one month, which may be extended by two further months where necessary, taking into account the complexity and number of requests (Article 12(3) GDPR). Requests to delete your data are normally completed within 7 days. Exercising your rights is free of charge, unless requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive.

9. Right to lodge a complaint

If you believe that the processing of your personal data infringes the GDPR, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the Member State of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement (Article 77 GDPR). In Italy, the competent authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, Piazza Venezia 11, 00187 Rome, www.garanteprivacy.it. You also have the right to an effective judicial remedy (Articles 78 and 79 GDPR).

10. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not use advertising, profiling or analytics cookies, and does not include third-party tracking tools or social media plug-ins that track you. It only uses technical storage in your browser (localStorage and, if you allow notifications or install the app, a service worker and a push subscription) which is strictly necessary to provide the service you request, such as remembering your ticket link. Under Article 122 of the Italian Privacy Code and the Garante’s guidelines of 10 June 2021, these technical tools do not require your consent. Where the human verification check is enabled, Cloudflare may set technical cookies that are strictly necessary to protect the request form. You can delete stored data at any time through your browser settings; doing so may mean you have to log in to your ticket page again.

11. Age

Our events and this website are for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that a request was made by someone under 18, we decline it and delete the related data.

12. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), personal links that are hard to guess, hashed passwords for crew accounts, access to the guest list limited to the people who need it, and limits on automated requests. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; if a personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will inform you without undue delay as required by Article 34 GDPR.

13. Links to other websites

The website contains links to other services, such as Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Drive and Google Maps. When you follow these links, the privacy policy of that service applies, and we are not responsible for how it processes your data.

14. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice, for example when we change how the website works or when the law changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. If the changes are significant, we will make this clear on the website or tell guests with an active request.
