Privacy.

We use your details to run our parties. Nothing else.

Never sold.

What we collect

Your request
Full name, Instagram, WhatsApp (optional) and your message (optional).
Rough location
City and country the request came from, from your connection. We don't store your IP address.
Your ticket
Status, ticket code, price, payment deadline and check-in time at the door.
Payment
We see your transfer in our own bank account to match it to your code. No bank or card details on this website.
Instagram DMs
Your username and message when you send us your code, to confirm the account is yours and reply.
On your phone
Your ticket link and what you typed, saved in your own browser so you don't type it again.

Why

To review your request, check you're 18+ and the Instagram is really yours, send your price and ticket, recognise you at the door, give returning guests priority, keep the nights safe and see roughly where guests come from. Instagram and WhatsApp are only used to contact you about your request and our parties.

Who sees it

Only the PerugiaParty team. The site runs on Cloudflare (hosting and database, servers in the EU and worldwide) and DMs go through Meta. They process data for us and don't use it themselves.

How long

As long as we organise parties, so we can recognise returning guests. Past guest lists are cleaned up regularly.

Delete your data

DM @perugiaparty on Instagram: delete my data + your Instagram handle. We delete everything within 7 days and confirm in the chat.
