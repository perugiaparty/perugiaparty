But why?

PerugiaParty is run by volunteers who want to make student life in Perugia a little more open and inclusive. Through music and a welcoming space, we bring people from Umbria and from all over the world together, Italian and Erasmus students alike, to share one night.

Good to know.

Are there Erasmus parties in Perugia?

Yes. PerugiaParty is made for university and Erasmus students in Perugia: a guest list, open bar, DJs and new people to meet. 18+.

Do I need to speak Italian?

No. The website, the tickets and our Instagram are in English, and the crowd is international.

How do I get on the guest list?

Request a place on perugiaparty.com, then send your code to @perugiaparty on Instagram. Once approved, pay by bank transfer and your ticket appears on your personal page.

Where is the party?

In Perugia’s city centre. The exact location appears on your ticket once we confirm your payment.

When is the next party?

Dates are announced on Instagram (@perugiaparty) and on perugiaparty.com.

Who can come?

Anyone aged 18 or over. Bring valid photo ID.
