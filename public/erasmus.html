Good to know.

I just arrived. What is PerugiaParty?

Private parties for university and Erasmus students in Perugia’s centre: open bar, DJs and new people, at a student price. Run by volunteers. 18+.

Do I need to speak Italian?

No. The website, your ticket and our Instagram are in English.

How do I join?

Request a place on this page, then DM your code to @perugiaparty on Instagram. We check every request and send you your ticket page.

I don’t have an Italian bank account.

No problem. A normal SEPA transfer in euro from any European account works. Use your name and code as the reference.

How do I get there?

It’s in the city centre, so you can walk from most student areas in town. The exact place is on your ticket after payment.

What do I bring?

Your ticket QR on your phone and a photo ID (passport or ID card). You must be 18+.

What are the house rules?
  1. On the list or not in. No list, no entry. Tickets are personal.
  2. Pay before you come. Bank transfer with your name and PP code as the reference.
  3. Pace yourself. One drink at a time. Don't waste them.
  4. Leave the music alone. Respect the vibe and whoever is playing.
  5. No mess. Use the bags. Leave the place as you found it.
  6. Respect the staff. They're the reason the night happens.
