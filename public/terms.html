Terms.

1. Who we are

PerugiaParty organises private, invite-only student nights in Perugia, Italy. It is run without profit by students. We are not a club, a venue or a nightlife company, and we do not have a permanent location: each night takes place at a venue that hosts us, under that venue's own licence and rules. People who work a night may receive a small compensation; nobody takes a profit out of the project.

These terms apply when you request a place, when you hold a ticket and while you are at one of our nights. By sending a request you accept them. If you do not accept them, do not send a request.

2. Requesting a place

Every night has its own guest list, and every night needs a new request, even if you have been before. A request is not a place: we check every request, and we can decline one without giving a reason. Your friends request their own places.

You must be 18 or older and bring photo identification. We may refuse entry to anyone who cannot show it.

3. Approval, price and payment

If your request is approved, your ticket page shows your price and the deadline for paying. You pay by bank transfer, using your name and your personal code as the payment reference. We must receive the payment before the deadline; if we have not received it by then, the place goes back to the list.

Your ticket page may offer you the option of adding a voluntary amount on top of the ticket price, to help cover what a night costs. It is entirely optional, it gives you no extra right or privilege, and the ticket price alone is enough.

4. Your ticket

A ticket is personal. It is valid for one person and for one night, and it cannot be given, sold or transferred to anyone else. At the door we check your QR code against your photo identification.

If you lose your link, open perugiaparty.com/ticket and log in with your Instagram username and your code.

5. Cancellation and refunds

Tickets are not refundable and cannot be transferred, including if you cannot come, arrive after the doors close or are refused entry for breaking these terms or the house rules.

If we cancel a night, or move it to another date and you cannot come on the new one, you get your money back. We contact you directly, through Instagram or the number you gave us, and we return the amount to the account the payment came from.

A voluntary contribution added on top of the ticket price is a gift to the project and is not refunded, unless the night itself is cancelled.

6. At the night

The house rules are part of these terms: you are on the list or you are not in; you pay before you come; one drink at a time; respect whoever is playing; leave the place as you found it; respect the staff.

The venue's own rules apply as well. The crew and the venue staff can refuse entry or ask you to leave, without a refund, if you are aggressive, if you harass anyone, if you damage the place, if you are too drunk to look after yourself, or if you break these terms.

Alcohol is served only to people aged 18 or over. We can refuse to serve anyone, and we always have water and a non-alcoholic option available.

7. Your things, and your own responsibility

You look after your own belongings. We have no cloakroom unless we say so for a particular night, and we are not responsible for anything lost, damaged or left behind.

You come to our nights of your own accord and you are responsible for your own behaviour. Nothing in these terms limits any right you have under Italian law as a consumer, or our responsibility where the law does not allow it to be limited.

8. Photographs and video

Photographs and short videos are taken at our nights and may be shared with guests or on our Instagram account. If you do not want to appear, tell a member of the crew at the night or send us a direct message, and we will avoid photographing you or remove the content where we can.

9. Changes

We may change the date, the venue, the theme, the music or anything else about a night. We tell you through your ticket page and on Instagram. We may also change these terms; the version on this page at the moment you send your request is the one that applies to you.

10. Contact, complaints and applicable law

Write to us by direct message at @perugiaparty on Instagram. We read everything and we answer personally.

Italian law applies to these terms. How we handle your personal data is explained in our privacy notice.
