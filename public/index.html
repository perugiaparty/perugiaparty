How it works.

Four steps, from request to the door.

  1. Request a place

    Fill in the form below with your Instagram. You get a personal link to your ticket page. Save it.

  2. Get approved

    We check every request. If you're in, your page shows your price, your PP code and the payment deadline.

  3. Pay

    Bank transfer with your full name and PP code as the reference. 72 hours for early bird and normal tickets, 24 hours for late ones.

  4. You're in

    Once we confirm your payment, your page shows the location, your QR code and a ticket to download. Bring it with photo ID.

Questions.

Who can come?

Anyone 18 or older. Every edition needs a fresh request and approval, even if you came before. Bring valid photo ID.

When do I see the price and location?

The price appears on your personal page once you're approved. The exact location appears after we confirm your payment.

How long do I have to pay?

72 hours for early bird and normal tickets, 24 hours for late tickets, unless your page says otherwise. An unpaid place goes back to the list when the deadline passes.

I lost my personal link.

On the same phone, tap "My ticket" at the top of this page. Otherwise DM us on Instagram and we'll send it again.

Can I bring a friend?

After you've been to an edition, you can ask to bring one friend per event. They still send their own request and get their own code.

Refunds and transfers?

Tickets are personal and can't be passed on. If you cancel, there's no refund. If we cancel, we'll contact you about the arrangements.
