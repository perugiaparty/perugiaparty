How it works.

Four steps, from request to the door.

  1. Request a place

    Fill in the form below with your Instagram. You get a personal link to your ticket page. Save it.

  2. Get approved

    We check every request. If you're in, your page shows your price, your PP code and the payment deadline.

  3. Pay

    Bank transfer with your full name and PP code as the reference. 72 hours for early bird and normal tickets, 24 hours for late ones.

  4. You're in

    Once we confirm your payment, your page shows the location, your QR code and a ticket to download. Bring it with photo ID.

Good to know.

Who can come?

Anyone aged 18 or over. Every event requires a new request and approval, even if you’ve joined us before. Bring valid photo ID.

Does applying guarantee a place?

We review every request. If approved, pay before your deadline to secure your place.

When can I see the price?

Your price appears on your personal ticket page once you’re approved.

When do I get the location?

The exact location appears on your ticket page once we confirm your payment.

When is payment due?

72 hours for early bird and normal tickets, or 24 hours for late tickets, unless your page says otherwise. Miss the deadline and your place becomes available again.

What if I lose my ticket link?

Go to perugiaparty.com/ticket and log in with your Instagram and your personal code (like H26-K7M4QX). On the phone you used to apply, “My ticket” at the top also works.

Can I bring a friend?

Your approval covers only you. Friends must apply separately and get their own approval and ticket.

Can I get a refund or transfer my ticket?

Tickets are personal and cannot be transferred. If you cancel, there’s no refund.

What if the event is cancelled?

We’ll contact you directly with the next steps and details about any refund arrangements.
