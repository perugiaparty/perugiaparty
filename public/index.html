How it works.

Four steps, from request to the door.

  1. Ask to join

    Fill in the request below and send it to us on Instagram.

  2. Get approved

    We review every request. If you're in, you get your price, your PP code and a payment deadline.

  3. Pay

    Bank transfer with your full name and PP code as the reference. 72 hours for early bird and normal tickets, 24 hours for late ones.

  4. You're in

    Once payment is confirmed you get your ticket and the location. Bring the ticket and photo ID.

Questions.

      

        
Who can come?
Anyone 18 or older. Every edition needs a fresh request and approval, even if you came before. Bring valid photo ID.

        
When do I see the price and location?
The price comes with your approval. The exact location comes after we confirm your payment.

        
How long do I have to pay?
72 hours for early bird and normal tickets, 24 hours for late tickets, unless we tell you otherwise. An unpaid place goes back to the list when the deadline passes.

        
Can I bring a friend?
After you've been to an edition, you can ask to bring one friend per event. They still send their own request and get their own code.

        
Refunds and transfers?
Tickets are personal and can't be passed on. If you cancel, there's no refund. If we cancel, we'll contact you about the arrangements.

        
What happens to my data?
This page stores nothing. What you send us in a DM stays in Instagram and is used only for the guest list. Ask us anytime to delete it.
