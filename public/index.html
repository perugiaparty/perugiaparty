Good to know.

Why is it so cheap?

Clubs in Perugia are expensive and far away, and the centre ends early. PerugiaParty is run by volunteers, so we keep the price low and every student can come.

Who can come?

Students, Erasmus and anyone aged 18+. Every party needs a new request, even if you came before. Friends request their own place. Bring photo ID.

How do I get on the list?

Request a place here, then DM your code to @perugiaparty on Instagram. We check every request; a request isn’t a guaranteed place.

How much is it and how do I pay?

Your price appears on your ticket page once you’re approved. Pay by bank transfer with your name and code as the reference.

Where is the party?

In Perugia’s city centre. The exact place appears on your ticket once we confirm your payment.

Lost my ticket, refunds?

Lost the link? Go to perugiaparty.com/ticket and log in with your Instagram and code. Tickets are personal and can’t be transferred or refunded. If we cancel, we contact you directly.

What are the house rules?
  1. On the list or not in. No list, no entry. Tickets are personal.
  2. Pay before you come. Bank transfer with your name and PP code as the reference.
  3. Pace yourself. One drink at a time. Don't waste them.
  4. Leave the music alone. Respect the vibe and whoever is playing.
  5. No mess. Use the bags. Leave the place as you found it.
  6. Respect the staff. They're the reason the night happens.
